## The cast plan settles on the virtual clock (task 2.5, design D4)

M9's check 1 settling test, repeated for `plan_cast` (`tests/integration/test_plan_graph_checks.py`,
`test_check1_cast_plans_settle_on_the_virtual_clock_and_repeat`): 100 group first-slot plans whose Jev answers land at
0.1 … 1.0 s, run twice on the virtual clock. Both runs settled with nothing left busy and gave identical plans; the 30
answers past the 700 ms deadline fell back to the least recently spoken (outcomes: 70 on time, 30 late).

## The follow plan settles on the virtual clock (task 4.6, design D4)

The same check for the follow plan (`test_check1_follow_plans_settle_on_the_virtual_clock_and_repeat`): 100 follow
plans, each started at a reply's stream end with its deadline at `turn.end` (stream end + 30 ms) + 400 ms. Jev answers
0.1 … 1.0 s after the start, and every tenth answer is parked and released only after the deadline. Both runs settled
with nothing left busy and gave identical plans: 40 picks (answers by 0.4 s) and 60 late, each with no second reply
(outcomes: 40 on time, 60 late).

## `F`: stream end to `turn.end` (task 4.7, design D9)

Measured in real time on Windows (the dev laptop, with the backend suite running alongside) over 100 scripted group
replies (`tests/integration/test_follow_timing.py`, `-s` prints it): **p50 12.3 ms, p90 13.3 ms, max 16.8 ms.** So the
follow plan, started at the stream's end, has about **412 ms** of Jev time before its deadline, not 400. D9's finding
stands: with Jev's turn_plan p50 at ≈ 450–530 ms (m9.md, latency re-run), more than half of follow plans are expected
late until the deadline follows measurement (G2; task 10.7 measures it).

## Follow-plan storms (task 4.8, design D2)

`tests/sessions/test_storms_m10.py`: Stop's cancel stormed on the job while it waits on a parked follow plan, and
again from the moment `finish` starts (cancels land in the guardrail check, the ledger reads and the `turn.end`
append). Both times the writer was free within 5 s, the follow plan's ledger row ended `cancelled`, no second reply
was made, and the log reduced.
