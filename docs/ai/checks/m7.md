## Finding: Jev bills its own prompt on top of ours

For each request, Jev reported **160–275 more input tokens** than our estimate of what we sent (bytes ÷ 3 over the state and the questions). The ratio runs from 1.3× on large states to 2.2× on small ones, so it behaves as a fixed per-request overhead, presumably Jev's own instructions. Group A's billing check (state billed once, $0.042/M exactly) still holds. It compared costs *between* requests, so the constant cancelled out.

Effects:
- **The eval runner** budgeted 1.5 × its estimate and stopped the first canary run after 18 of 20 items. Fixed in M7: `horizon/evals/runner.py` adds `JEV_REQUEST_OVERHEAD_TOKENS = 300` to its estimates, and `--start N` lets an incomplete run finish without paying again for the items already answered.
- **The gateway's preflight estimate** (`Gateway.decision_estimate`, design D11, group A: "stays as designed") under-reserves by about 200 tokens per decision, about $0.0000084 at $0.042/M. It doesn't affect correctness: the ledger records the provider's actual cost, and the reservation is released at once. **Not changed in M7**, because group A locked it. The proposed fix is to add the same per-request constant to `decision_estimate` and update the D11 test. It is left for the user to approve.
