**Read by hand** (every failure):

- **Supported.** Of memory-2's 14 flagged lines, 9 are fine: the detail is in the messages or in that character's

  - 2 are a mild overreach ("Kai confirmed the booking").

- **Lost.** Of the 9 flagged, 3 are a changed detail given its new value, which isn't a loss. Real losses:

## What M13 found

1. **The writer works where it matters most:**
   - every call is valid;
   - changed facts are rewritten, not duplicated (92 %);
   - untouched lines are left alone (503/503);
   - nothing secret is stored;
   - small talk writes nothing.

   Its weak spots are fine detail:
   - about 11–17 % of rewrites lose a still-true detail (a past event, a severity);
   - about 3 % of lines carry a detail only another character's list held. One call sees every present character's
     list, and a prompt rule didn't fix it.

   Both are accepted, and recorded as known limits.
2. **A guard needs changed values in its set.** v1, tuned without them, held back almost half of the real changes.
   Rewording it ("a detail the conversation changed counts as kept") and adding changed and changed-lossy items fixed
   it at `t_keep` 0.35.
3. **Cosine can't tell a rewording from an extension.** Rewordings sit at 0.81–0.97 and extensions at 0.97–0.99, so
   `dupCosine` became a pre-filter, and the new Jev noul `same_fact` decides (121/122 by policy).
4. **Search, not Jev, limits recall.**
   - In-session, dense-only lines put the needed line in the top 3 for 97 % of probes, and Jev keeps 93 %.
   - For the overflow, indirect questions rank the needed memory too low: 25 % in the top 3, 80 % in the checked 6,
     and 62 % kept with the standout rule.
5. **Live, recall mostly runs on keywords.** The query embedding from Malaysia is 0.9–1.3 s on every OpenRouter
   provider, and the agent path waits 200 ms, so 49 of 59 timed turns had no vector. The dense-lines gain holds only
   when the vector arrives in time.
6. **Recall's Jev tail.** One `recall` per candidate is p50 ≈ 0.8 s, but the slowest of 6 is p90 1.6 s and p99 3.2 s.
   With both gates, recall ends up to 0.94 s after the documents at p90. `checkMs` stays 1,500, and the tail is
   recorded for M15.
7. **Two judges needed context:**
   - `judge_memory_lost` must know what the conversation changed;
   - `judge_over_mention` must see the session's last lines.

   Both pass B4 at v2. The supported judge passes B4 but over-flags merged lines, so its rate is a ceiling.
8. **Model output needs tolerant checks.** The rewrite call writes `"about": 0` for the user. Dropping those lines lost
   about 9 % of memory-1's writing until phase 10 read them as the user.
9. **Bugs found and fixed on the way:**
   - Forget's vector zeroing failed on vec0 for any memory with a vector (an M5 bug);
   - parallel children broke the virtual clock's activity token (`vclock.together`);
   - planted IDs must be contract-valid;
   - an e2e test flaky since before M13.
10. **Cost.** The whole phase-10 evaluation cost $0.152 against ≈ $0.27. A pause run costs about what doc 09 M13
    estimated, and takes p50 2.5 s.

- **For M15:**
