# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: New session opening
A created session SHALL start `active`, with the MockClient's defaults for title, config, state and music policy per mode. Its first events SHALL be a `session.state` snapshot and one `energy` event per participant. A 1:1 session SHALL then greet, a debate SHALL start its first phase, and a watch SHALL post its premise and start playing, each after the shared timing table's delay. A 1:1 greeting SHALL be the character's stored greeting, word for word and at no cost, when the greeting is not empty and no other non-seed, non-fork 1:1 session holds a message written by that character; otherwise it SHALL be generated by the profile's turn engine.

#### Scenario: 1:1 greeting
- **WHEN** a 1:1 session with Amara is created and 6 seconds of clock time pass
- **THEN** the session holds exactly one character message, `complete`, and the snapshot returned by create already reflected the opening events

#### Scenario: First chat uses the approved greeting
- **WHEN** the first 1:1 session with a newly approved character opens
- **THEN** its greeting message's text equals the character's stored greeting, and no ledger row or `energy` event follows it

#### Scenario: Seed recordings and forks don't count
- **WHEN** Amara has only seed sessions and a "Continue live" fork of one, and a new 1:1 session with her opens
- **THEN** the greeting is her stored greeting, word for word

#### Scenario: Second chat is generated
- **WHEN** a character already wrote a message in an earlier non-seed, non-fork 1:1 session, and a new 1:1 session opens with a key set
- **THEN** the greeting is generated by the profile's turn engine and billed as a reply

#### Scenario: Asleep first chat doesn't count
- **WHEN** a character's first 1:1 session only got an asleep note, and a second 1:1 session opens after she recovers
- **THEN** the second session's greeting is her stored greeting, word for word

### Requirement: Fork a recording
`sessions.forkSeedSession(id, atSeq?)` SHALL need a usable key, and SHALL create a new non-seed session with `continuedFrom: id` from the source's events up to `atSeq`. If a turn was streaming at `atSeq`, the cut SHALL extend to that turn's `turn.end`. Event and message IDs SHALL be renamed deterministically and events renumbered from 1, as the MockClient does. The source session SHALL be unchanged. When the fork's history is over its mode's window, its summary task SHALL start at creation (see `conversation-context`).

#### Scenario: Fork a debate at a third of the way
- **WHEN** `ses_seedDebate4Day` is forked at the `seq` a third of the way through its events
- **THEN** the fork is non-seed, `continuedFrom` is `ses_seedDebate4Day`, no message is `streaming`, it has fewer messages than the source, and the source's event count is unchanged

#### Scenario: Long fork is summarised before the first message
- **WHEN** a session whose history is past its window is forked at its end, and no message is sent
- **THEN** a `rolling_summary` call is made for the fork, and a `rolling` summary row for the fork exists once it lands
